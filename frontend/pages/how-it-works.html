How It Works

A clear, guided process from start to ownership.

Every step is explained before it happens — so you always know what's next and why.

01

Consultation

We start with a free conversation about your goals, budget and timeline. No properties are discussed until I understand what you're actually trying to achieve.

02

Property selection

Based on that conversation, I shortlist a small number of properties that genuinely fit — not a long list to choose from, but the right few.

03

Due diligence

Before you ever visit a site, I verify survey plans, excision status, and title history. Any issues are disclosed upfront, not discovered later.

04

Inspection

You visit the property in person (or via video for diaspora clients), see the boundaries, and ask any questions on-site.

05

Documentation

I walk you through exactly what paperwork you'll receive and when — Deed of Assignment, Survey Plan, and Governor's Consent status.

06

Payment

Flexible payment plans are available on most properties. Every instalment and deadline is agreed in writing before you pay anything.

07

Ownership

You receive your documentation, and I remain available for any follow-up — title registration support, development advice, or your next investment.
