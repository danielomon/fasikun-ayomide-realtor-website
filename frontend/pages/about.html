My Story

How I got here

I got into real estate the same way a lot of my clients come to me now — curious about building wealth, but wary of an industry with a reputation for pressure tactics and unclear documentation. Early in my career, I watched people close to me lose money on land that was never properly titled. That experience shaped everything about how I work today.

Rather than starting with "here's what's available," I start with "here's what you're trying to achieve." Every recommendation that follows is built around that — not around what's easiest for me to sell.

My Approach

How I actually help investors

I ask before I recommend

Your budget, timeline, and risk appetite shape every property I bring to you — not the other way around.

I show you the paperwork

Survey plans, excision documents and title status are shared and explained before you commit to anything.

I stay after the sale

Documentation, title transfer and any post-purchase questions remain my responsibility, not something you're left to chase alone.

Why clients trust me with their first investment

Because I treat every transaction as if it were my own money. If a property isn't right for you, I'll tell you — even if it costs me a sale.
